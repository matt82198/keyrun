# 01 — What makes WoW Mythic+ fun, and what do its players hate?

## 1. The question

What makes the Mythic+ key/timer/affix/loot loop fun, and which parts of it does the community
actively hate — with enough specificity to know what Keyrun should steal, adapt, or avoid.

## 2. What the best games do

1. **WoW Mythic+ — the timer against a scaling key.** A party runs a dungeon whose difficulty is
   set by the keystone's level; beating the internal boss/trash timer upgrades the key, missing it
   downgrades it. The timer converts a dungeon clear from "content consumed" into "a performance
   graded pass/fail," which is why it generates tension every pull instead of only at the final
   boss. Game director Ion Hazzikostas has framed M+ design choices (e.g. one shared battle-rez
   timer for the whole instance since patch 7.3.5) around treating the *dungeon as a single
   encounter*, not a string of unrelated fights — the timer is what makes that framing true.
   Source: https://www.wowhead.com/news/bellular-patch-10-1-interview-with-ion-hazzikostas-adding-new-mythic-affixes-and-331833 ;
   mechanic description: https://conquestcapped.com/guides/wow/mythic-plus-timer/
2. **WoW Mythic+ — infinite key-level ladder on a fixed dungeon pool.** Same handful of dungeons,
   difficulty is a dial (key level) instead of a discrete tier list. This lets skill expression and
   status be continuous rather than binary (cleared/not cleared), so there's always a next rung.
   Source: https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Mythic+_affix
3. **WoW Mythic+ — the interrupt/kick economy.** Groups pre-assign who kicks which spell on a pull
   instead of everyone reflexively kicking the first cast they see ("interrupt overlap"); advanced
   players practice "cast-bar patience," waiting until a cast is 70-80% complete to maximize denial
   value. This turns a single button into a running coordination puzzle across the whole pull.
   Source: https://thelighthouseworks.com/wow-mythic-plus-interrupt-guide-build-a-kick-plan-even-with-strangers/ ;
   https://tanknotes.com/mythic-plus/dungeon-tips/
4. **WoW Mythic+ — pull size and routing as the real skill test.** Tanks decide how many trash
   packs to chain-pull before resetting threat/cooldowns; bigger pulls are faster but riskier, and
   route choice (which packs, which order, skips) is planned before the run and adjusted live. This
   is the single biggest skill-expression axis in the mode — not gear, not reaction time to one
   mechanic, but the standing decision of "how much can we safely bite off." Source:
   https://tanknotes.com/mythic-plus/dungeon-tips/
5. **WoW Mythic+ — Great Vault as a deferred, choice-based reward.** Instead of loot dropping
   directly, weekly M+ activity unlocks up to 3 reward slots and the player *chooses one item from a
   set* at reset. Picking a reward, not just receiving one, adds an anticipation window (all week)
   and an agency moment (the choice itself) on top of the loot. Source:
   https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/great-vault-guide ;
   https://www.method.gg/guides/dragonflight-season-4-end-of-dungeon-and-great-vault-rewards
6. **WoW Mythic+ — end-of-dungeon loot as the immediate reward.** A smaller, separate roll happens
   the instant the boss dies, decoupled from the weekly vault — so the loop pays out at both the
   session grain (every run) and the weekly grain (vault), not just one or the other. Source:
   https://mythic-store.com/blog/the-great-vault-explained-complete-guide-for-wow-midnight-season-2
7. **WoW Mythic+ — weekly rotating affixes force route/comp adaptation.** A modifier (or set of
   modifiers) changes every week, so a "solved" route or comp goes stale on a schedule and players
   must re-plan. Source: https://raider.io/news/740-midnight-mythic-plus-affixes
8. **WoW Mythic+ — Xal'atath's Bargain (affixes with upside, not just downside).** The War Within
   redesign replaced pure-penalty affixes with modifiers that also grant a player-usable buff,
   explicitly to make the weekly modifier something players *opt into using well*, not just endure.
   Source: https://www.mmo-champion.com/content/12398-Affix-System-Updates-in-The-War-Within
9. **WoW Mythic+ — Raider.IO score as a persistent status ladder.** A single portable number
   summarizes "what this character has cleared, at what level, in time," and functions as the de
   facto currency for group access — separate from in-game achievements or item level. Source:
   https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow/guides/raider-io-addon-guide/ ;
   https://blizzardwatch.com/2020/05/05/important-gear-scores-forming-groups-world-warcraft/
10. **Fellowship / Dark and Darker — queue-based dungeon matchmaking.** Rather than manually
    assembling a party from a browsable list, players queue and get matched (or vote among a small
    set of options), removing the "read 40 LFG posts, apply, get rejected" friction entirely.
    Source: https://steamcommunity.com/app/2352620/discussions/0/624436764982906738/

## 3. What players hate

1. **The death timer penalty.** Each death subtracts seconds from the clock; this was raised from 5s
   to 15s and became one of the most complained-about mechanics because it can single-handedly lose
   an otherwise-successful key, and lands hardest on roles (healers, melee) more exposed to
   mechanics. Blizzard later removed the death penalty entirely at the lowest key levels (+2/+3) in
   response. Source: https://eu.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/15s-death-penalty-in-m-needs-to-go/541746 ;
   https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/news/no-more-death-time-penalty-for-mythic-2-and-3/ ;
   https://www.wowhead.com/news/timer-penalty-for-deaths-removed-from-2-and-3-mythic-keystones-375646
2. **Exclusionary affixes.** Sanguine, Bolstering, Raging, Bursting, Reckless, Focused, Attuned, and
   Thorned were all retired; Blizzard's own stated reason was that "applying bonuses unevenly to
   players feels exclusionary regardless of overall impact" — i.e., a modifier that only some specs
   or comps can play around is a design failure, not a feature. Source:
   https://www.mmo-champion.com/content/12398-Affix-System-Updates-in-The-War-Within
3. **Raider.IO gatekeeping in the group finder.** PUG leaders set score requirements far above what
   a given key level needs (a +5 group demanding a 1000+ score), locking out the exact players who
   need lower keys to build score in the first place — a long-running, still-active forum complaint
   thread. Source: https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/raider-io-is-ruining-mythic-group-finder/3834
4. **No real matchmaking — manual PUG assembly.** Group finder is a browsable list, not a queue;
   players must scan, apply, and get accepted/rejected per group, and the toxicity/gatekeeping above
   is a direct consequence of that manual, reputation-gated assembly step. Source (same thread):
   https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/raider-io-is-ruining-mythic-group-finder/3834
5. **Loot flattening at the top.** End-of-dungeon loot and vault item level both cap at key level
   +10 — a +15 clear rewards identically to a +10 — so the extrinsic reward stops scaling with
   difficulty well before the skill ceiling does. Source:
   https://mythic-store.com/blog/the-great-vault-explained-complete-guide-for-wow-midnight-season-2
6. **Affix churn without payoff.** Repeated affix reworks (2023 Season 2 retiring Quaking/
   Grievous/Volcanic, the 2024 War Within overhaul, ongoing 2025-26 tuning) drew both praise for
   clearer counterplay and complaints that the system still feels generic/unmemorable to longtime
   players. Sources: https://blizzardwatch.com/2023/05/09/mythic-affixes-dragonflight-season-2/ ;
   https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/bring-back-the-variety-of-affixes-dear-blizzard/2199086

## 4. Steal / adapt / avoid

**Steal**
- The timer-against-a-scaling-difficulty spine itself — it's the load-bearing "why does clearing feel
  like a pass/fail performance" mechanic, and it costs nothing in art (a number and a clock).
- End-of-run choice-of-reward (vault-style autonomy) — pick 1-of-N, cheap as text/shape UI, adds an
  agency beat on top of the drop itself.
- Interrupt/kick pre-assignment as coordination skill — put the interrupt inside the ≤5 rotational
  abilities so *when* to use it is a real decision, directly serving the synergy-first goal.
- A persistent status number (Raider.IO analog) as the retention hook and future matchmaking input.

**Adapt**
- Pull-size/routing skill expression — Keyrun is top-down 2D and bots-first, so make the read visual
  (pull radius, path shapes on screen) instead of WoW's memorized-3D-geography skill floor.
- Weekly rotating affix — keep the *idea* (a modifier forces re-planning) but run exactly ONE active
  modifier at a time, never stacked; a ≤5-ability kit can't spare attention for simultaneous
  Bargain+Fortified+Guile-style layering.
- Death penalty — keep a *soft* time cost per death (not WoW's 15s), informed directly by §3.1: don't
  let one mistake alone flip a clearable run to a failed one.

**Avoid**
- Stacking multiple simultaneous affixes (WoW's Bargain + Fortified/Tyrannical + Guile at once) —
  too much simultaneous state for a tight ability/keybind budget.
- Score-gated manual group finder with no matchmaking — the single most-hated social friction in the
  source material; if/when Keyrun adds pugging, queue-and-match (Fellowship/Dark and Darker-style)
  from day one.
- Any modifier that only some kits can play around — Blizzard's own retirement rationale for
  Sanguine/Bolstering/etc.; test every affix candidate against all classes before it ships.
- A hard reward cap that flattens incentive above a difficulty threshold.

## 5. Verdict

Keep the timer-against-a-scaling-key as Keyrun's spine — it's the proven source of pull-by-pull
tension. Pair it with a same-session choice-of-reward at run end (a vault-lite, since Keyrun has no
weekly cadence) so the loop pays out immediately and with agency. Run exactly one active difficulty
modifier at a time, never stacked, and pre-test every modifier against all classes — the community's
loudest, most consistent hate is exclusionary or stacked affixes, and Keyrun's ≤5-ability budget
can't afford divided attention anyway. If pugging is ever added, queue-and-match from day one;
manual LFG + a gatekeeping score is the single ugliest failure mode in the source material.
