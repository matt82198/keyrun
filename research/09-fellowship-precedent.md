# 09 — Fellowship precedent

## 1. The question

Fellowship (Chief Rebel, published by Arc Games, early access Oct 16 2025) is the closest existing
precedent to Keyrun — a standalone co-op "Mythic+"-only dungeon game with no open world. What exactly
does it ship, what do reviewers/players praise vs. call half-baked, how did its playerbase trend, and
what gap should Keyrun exploit?

## 2. What Fellowship ships, and what's praised

- **Classes/heroes.** Launched with 9 heroes across Tank/Healer/DPS (the "holy trinity"); grew to 11 by
  the guide cited below (3 Tank, 3 Healer, 5 DPS), each with a distinct mechanic (builder/spender, pet,
  combo, charge-timing). [MMORPG.com preview](https://www.mmorpg.com/previews/fellowship-early-access-preview-chief-rebels-moda-builds-a-game-that-starts-at-the-finish-line-2000136324),
  [Icy Veins beginner guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/fellowship/news/fellowship-beginner-guide-everything-you-need-to-know/).
- **Ability count / keybinds.** Each hero starts with ~10 core abilities and unlocks more via dungeon
  clears, but all of it funnels into a single 16-slot hotbar (1-5, Shift+1-6, 6-8, Shift+7-8), plus
  separate bindings for interrupt select/trigger, 4 ally-select keys, 5 mark-unit keys, 5 mark-location
  keys, mount, spirit ability, HUD toggle — 45+ distinct bindable actions in total.
  [Fextralife Controls](https://fellowship.wiki.fextralife.com/Controls),
  [MMORPG.com preview](https://www.mmorpg.com/previews/fellowship-early-access-preview-chief-rebels-moda-builds-a-game-that-starts-at-the-finish-line-2000136324).
- **Dungeon loop / queueing.** Pick hero → queue (Quickplay, no gear reward, ~10-15 min; or Challenge,
  ranked 4-player, timed) → kill mobs to a 100% kill-score threshold across branching paths → boss →
  loot. Camera is third-person over-the-shoulder; movement/controls are "standard third-person action"
  and reviewers call them smooth. [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2352620/Fellowship/),
  [MMORPG.com review](https://www.mmorpg.com/reviews/fellowship-early-access-review-2000136475),
  [Console Creatures review](https://www.consolecreatures.com/fellowship-review/) (7/10 — "fast-paced and
  dynamic combat" with "smooth controls").
- **Key levels / affixes / leagues.** Leagues: Contender → Adept → Champion → Paragon, each with +1 to
  +4 internal difficulty; "curses" (affixes) start at Adept; capstone (triple-boss) dungeons unlock at
  +7; Eternal/Endless mode scales infinitely once Paragon is cleared, and gear rewards cap out there so
  the challenge becomes purely skill-based. [Icy Veins guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/fellowship/news/fellowship-beginner-guide-everything-you-need-to-know/),
  [GameRant on Endless Mode](https://gamerant.com/fellowship-endless-mode-endgame-explained/).
- **Progression / gear / talents.** No character leveling — power is gear item level, tempering (gold +
  materials, capped by dungeon difficulty), and gems; talent trees award points that unlock flexible
  rows (rows can be split 2-3 ways) with free, frictionless respec between runs.
  [Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/fellowship/news/fellowship-beginner-guide-everything-you-need-to-know/),
  [BuyBoost Season 3 overview](https://buyboost.com/news/fellowship/fellowship-season3).
- **Monetization.** $24.99 at early access launch, cosmetic-only microtransactions (mounts, skins,
  supporter packs), no gameplay impact; devs stated it would stay in EA "approximately 6 months."
  [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2352620/Fellowship/).
- **Design intent (dev interviews).** Community director Hamish Bode: "it's taking our favorite bits of
  MMOs, action RPGs, and MOBAs." The stated goal is to let players "skip the grind" and start at what
  used to be the endgame, with lowering time-to-dungeon as an explicit ongoing priority (Quickplay/press-
  Play matchmaking). [GameRant interview](https://gamerant.com/interview-fellowship-rpg-grind-endless-scalability-worldbuilding/),
  [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/fellowship-is-a-new-type-of-co-op-rpg-thats-all-dungeons-without-the-filler-coming-to-early-access/).
- **Reception headline.** Steam overall 78% positive across 10,002 reviews; MMORPG.com review-in-progress
  score 75 ("the core gameplay loop is wonderful and feels great for long-time MMO dungeon fans... rough
  edges to be smoothed out"); OpenCritic aggregate ~40% across 5 outlets is dragged down by outlier harsh
  scores but NoobFeed (79/100) and Console Creatures (7/10) both call it good-but-unfinished.
  [MMORPG.com review](https://www.mmorpg.com/reviews/fellowship-early-access-review-2000136475),
  [OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/15565/fellowship/reviews),
  [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2352620/Fellowship/).

## 3. What reviewers and players call half-baked or missing

- **"Half-baked" is a literal player quote.** A negative Steam review: "Half-baked release, half-baked
  development path, inspiring 0 confidence in the players," criticizing optimization, server stability,
  and derivativeness from WoW. [Steam reviews](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2352620/reviews/).
- **Loot "really falls apart."** No trash drops or mid-dungeon gold; most drops don't upgrade a
  character; too few high-value drops to produce a reward rush. Chief Rebel itself confirms this by
  entirely rebuilding itemization for Season 3 — separating item level from item quality because the two
  "were previously tied together in a way that limited build diversity."
  [BuyBoost Season 3 overview](https://buyboost.com/news/fellowship/fellowship-season3).
- **No mid-run backfill.** "If a player drops out mid-dungeon, there is no way to queue up while still
  in the dungeon to replace the dropped player," forcing abandonment. [MMORPG.com review](https://www.mmorpg.com/reviews/fellowship-early-access-review-2000136475).
- **Content too thin to carry a whole game.** Players report repetition inside "less than an hour," and
  criticize a season built on ~8 dungeons run over and over. Fellowship's own reviewers frame this as the
  price of "cutting the fat" — "the diet gets bland fast" outside a dedicated Mythic+ audience.
  [Steam discussion: "A Game That Starts at the Finish Line"](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2352620/discussions/0/624436409752793497/).
- **Classes feel bland despite the ability count.** Community consensus in the Asmongold-reaction thread:
  "classes or heroes felt very bland and get boring very quickly," and the game is "a simple, mindless
  'abridged version' of larger MMOs with extremely little content." Notably this criticism survives
  Fellowship having ~10 abilities/hero and a 16-slot bar — button count did not buy perceived depth.
  [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2352620/discussions/0/624436409752641592/).
- **No built-in voice chat, weak pings, shaky matchmaking.** Coordination with strangers is "frustrating,"
  and missions fail from comms breakdowns rather than skill gaps — a sharp problem in a game whose core
  loop is queue-with-strangers. [Console Creatures review](https://www.consolecreatures.com/fellowship-review/).
- **Streamer verdict split but pointed.** Asmongold called it a "worse version of mythic+" (though he
  dislikes M+ generally and played an earlier build); community pushback largely conceded the bland-hero
  critique while disputing his overall dismissal. [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2352620/discussions/0/624436409752641592/).

**Player-count trajectory** (SteamDB/SteamCharts): peak **43,841** concurrent Oct 19 2025 (launch week);
monthly averages then fell off a cliff — Oct 2025 ~23,137 avg → Nov ~10,046 (-57%) → Dec ~2,515 (-75%) →
Jan 2026 ~846 (-66%), a brief content-patch bounce to ~2,240 in Feb, a slide to ~157 by May, a Season-
related rebound to ~2,680 in July, then back down to ~653 in Aug and ~377 in the last 30 days — a
>99% drop from peak with only short-lived content-patch recoveries, the classic shape of a strong-concept,
thin-content-cadence early access title. [SteamDB charts](https://steamdb.info/app/2352620/charts/),
[SteamCharts](https://steamcharts.com/app/2352620).

## 4. Steal / adapt / avoid, and the gap

**Steal** (fits Keyrun's constraints):
- Queue → dungeon → boss → simple return-to-hub loop with no open world, no leveling, no quest text —
  proof this loop alone can review at 75-78% positive on concept. [MMORPG.com review](https://www.mmorpg.com/reviews/fellowship-early-access-review-2000136475).
- Free, frictionless talent respec between runs so players can experiment without punishment. [Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/fellowship/news/fellowship-beginner-guide-everything-you-need-to-know/).
- Affixes/curses layered onto difficulty tiers rather than into every run from the start, so new players
  aren't hit with modifier complexity immediately. [Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/fellowship/news/fellowship-beginner-guide-everything-you-need-to-know/).

**Adapt** (use the idea, not the scale):
- Cosmetic-only monetization, but Keyrun is a POC with no shop at all yet — defer, don't build.
- Leagues-as-difficulty-ladder is sound; Keyrun's MVP should keep this to 2-3 tiers, not four leagues
  x four sub-tiers, given the ≤5-ability/synergy-first scope.

**Avoid** (directly caused Fellowship's "half-baked" reputation):
- A 16-slot hotbar plus 45+ total bindings that still produced "bland" heroes — proves more buttons is
  not how you buy perceived depth. Keyrun's ≤5 rotational abilities / ≤10 keybinds constraint should be
  read as a feature, not a limitation to route around.
- Itemization that shipped with item level and item quality conflated, needing a full Season 3 rebuild —
  an admission the launch reward system didn't work. Keyrun's "simple character screen" must get gear
  legibility right on day one, not iterate into it later.
- No mid-run backfill — a queue-into-run game that can't refill its own queue mid-run.

**The gap.** People who like Fellowship's *premise* (Mythic+ without the MMO) consistently want two
things it doesn't deliver: (1) mechanical distinctiveness per class that survives past the first hour —
not more abilities, but abilities/talents that read as synergy with teammates rather than a personal
rotation to memorize; and (2) a reward loop that pays off reliably at simple-character-screen scale,
instead of "most drops don't matter." Both gaps map directly onto Keyrun's stated constraints:
**synergy-first** is exactly the lever Fellowship needed but didn't pull (it had bloat, not synergy), and
**simple character screen** is exactly the promise Fellowship broke (it built a complex itemization
system that still felt unrewarding, then had to rebuild it). Keyrun's **≤5 rotational abilities / ≤10
keybinds** constraint is validated directly: Fellowship had 3x the ability surface and 4x+ the keybind
surface and still got called bland — so cutting bloat doesn't cost Keyrun the "depth" Fellowship has, because
Fellowship's own players say it didn't have that depth in the first place. **Queue-into-run** needs to
explicitly solve backfill, which Fellowship shipped without.

**Proposal — five things Keyrun should do differently from Fellowship, and why:** *(proposal)*
1. *(proposal)* Design synergy into the ability list itself (cross-class combo triggers, not just
   personal builder/spender chains) — Fellowship had abilities but not synergy, and "bland heroes despite
   depth" was its top community complaint.
2. *(proposal)* Ship mid-run backfill/late-join in the queue-into-run system from the first playable
   build — Fellowship's lack of it was a named, specific defect, not a subjective one.
3. *(proposal)* Make every dungeon clear feel like visible progress on the simple character screen (no
   "most drops don't upgrade you") — Fellowship's loot system was reviewers' most consistent complaint
   and required a full itemization rebuild to address.
4. *(proposal)* Hold the ≤10 keybind budget to include *all* interactive systems (marking, pings,
   interrupts), not just rotation — Fellowship's 45+ bindings show how fast secondary systems balloon
   past the core kit if not budgeted together.
5. *(proposal)* Build minimal in-run communication signaling (pings at least) as a day-one feature, since
   queue-into-run means strangers by default — Fellowship shipped without built-in voice and a weak ping
   system, and that was named as a direct cause of failed runs.

## 5. Verdict

Fellowship proves the isolated Mythic+ loop is reviewable and fun on concept alone (75-78% positive,
"the core gameplay loop is wonderful") but its post-launch collapse (43,841 peak to sub-1,000 average
within three months) and its own Season 3 itemization rebuild show the half-baked read is earned: bloat
(16-slot bar, 45+ bindings) without synergy, and complex gearing without reliable reward. Keyrun should
adopt the bare loop and free respec, but treat its own constraints — ≤5 abilities, ≤10 keybinds, synergy-
first, simple character screen, backfill-capable queue-into-run — as the direct fix for Fellowship's
named defects, not as risk to manage around them.
