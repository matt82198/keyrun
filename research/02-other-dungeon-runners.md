# 02 — What do other co-op dungeon/instance games do better than WoW's Mythic+?

## The question

Across FFXIV, GW2, Lost Ark, Diablo IV/PoE, Dark and Darker, Deep Rock Galactic, and Monster Hunter, what makes
their dungeon/instance loops fun in ways Mythic+ isn't, and what should Keyrun steal, adapt, or avoid?

## What the best games do

1. **FFXIV — Duty Finder queues everyone, including for progression content.** Normal (non-Savage) raids and most
   dungeons are queueable via Duty Finder even at endgame; Savage requires only level cap, MSQ access, and an item
   level floor — no separate key/score gate. This removes the "prove yourself before you're allowed to try"
   friction that M+ keys create. Why it works: lowering the entry cost to *attempt* content separates the skill
   check from the social gatekeeping check. Source: [Duty Finder — Final Fantasy Wiki](https://finalfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Duty_Finder); [FFXIV raiding structure discussion](https://www.mmo-champion.com/threads/2287541-WoW-players-that-switched-to-FFXIV-I-need-your-opinions/page5).

2. **FFXIV — job design compresses each class to a small, legible kit.** Ability "bloat" removals (e.g., Samurai's
   Hissatsu: Kaiten) were explicitly justified by the dev team as reducing button count so the remaining buttons
   read clearly on screen during a mechanic-dense fight. Why it works: fewer buttons means more of the player's
   attention budget goes to reading the boss, not managing the bar — this is close to Keyrun's own ≤5-ability
   constraint. Source: [FFXIV job simplification community thread, Square Enix forums](https://forum.square-enix.com/ffxiv/threads/478635-Inevitable-Job-Simplifications).

3. **FFXIV — Savage mechanics are telegraphed, not hidden.** Ground-AoE markers, stack/spread head-markers with
   numbered priority, and castbar timing are all visible and consistent rules players learn once and reuse across
   fights. Why it works: a mechanic you can *see coming* and solve with positioning turns a "gotcha" into a puzzle;
   failure feels like a player error, not a random roll. Source: [Raiding Fundamentals: Unconveyed Info in FFXIV's engine, AkhMorning](https://www.akhmorning.com/resources/raiding-fundamentals/); [FFXIV Marker Mechanics Guide](https://www.phoenixuprising.net/ffxiv-marker-mechanics-guide/).

4. **GW2 — the holy trinity is de-emphasized, not load-bearing.** ArenaNet's founding design blog argued against
   the tank/healer/DPS split, pushing healing toward proactive positioning (block, block-heal, reflect) rather than
   reactive HP-topping, and letting any profession contribute damage, support, or mitigation. Why it works: it
   removes the "nobody wants to heal/tank" queue-time bottleneck and makes every party slot a DPS-adjacent choice.
   Source: ["GW2: official Blogpost about the Holy Trinity", summarized on mmo-champion](https://www.mmo-champion.com/threads/1078544-GW2-official-Blogpost-about-the-Holy-Trinity-must-read); [Combat mode — GW2 Wiki](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Combat_mode).

5. **GW2 — combo fields turn overlapping abilities into bonus effects.** A field (fire, water, light, etc.) laid on
   the ground combines with a "finisher" ability (blast, projectile, whirl) from *any* class to produce an effect
   neither ability has alone (blast a water field for party healing, blast a fire field for might). Why it works:
   synergy is systemic and improvised rather than a fixed rotation — two classes that never "designed" to combo
   still can. Source: [Guild Wars 2 Combo System Breakdown, Medium](https://medium.com/@ozgr.srgz/guild-wars-2-combo-system-breakdown-0863b9d6e25d).

6. **GW2 — boon support roles compress "healer" and "buffer" into one seat.** Modern meta favors builds that
   provide Quickness or Alacrity (party-wide haste/cooldown-reduction) *while* healing or dealing damage, instead
   of a dedicated buff-bot. Why it works: it keeps party size small and every seat doing 1.5 jobs, avoiding the
   "bring a class because of one button" trap. Source: [GW2 Builds Explained: Boons, Roles & Synergy, BoostRoom](https://boostroom.com/blog/gw2-builds-explained-boons-roles-and-party-synergy); [Chronomancer Boon Support build, Metabattle](https://metabattle.com/wiki/Build:Chronomancer_-_Boon_Support_Power_DPS).

7. **Lost Ark — stagger and counter turn boss damage into a shared secondary bar.** Most skills contribute some
   "stagger" value to a boss's purple bar; breaking it staggers the boss and stacks a permanent damage-taken debuff.
   Counter windows (boss glows blue before a big attack) let a well-timed skill interrupt it entirely. Why it
   works: it gives the party a *second* win condition besides raw DPS, one that rewards using off-rotation skills
   and coordinating who counters. Source: [Lost Ark Mechanics Guide: Counter, Stagger, Weak Point, MMOGah](https://www.mmogah.com/news/lost-ark/lost-ark-mechanics-guide-counter-stagger-weak-point); [General Guardian Raid Guide, Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/lost-ark/resources/general-raid-guide).

8. **Diablo IV / PoE — the loop is judged on constant density, not walk time.** PoE's "map juicing" stacks
   pack-size, rarity, and quantity modifiers so a run is packed wall-to-wall with enemies; Diablo IV's Season-2+
   dungeon rework cut backtracking and clustered objectives so players are "almost constantly fighting." Why it
   works: dead travel time is the single most-cited killer of a loot loop's fun — both games treat empty corridors
   as a bug to be juiced or patched away. Source: [How to Juice Your Maps in PoE, Esports Edition](https://esportsedition.com/featured/poe-juice/); ["The codex is easier to fill up": D4 Season 2 dungeon changes, Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/mmo/the-codex-easier-fill-up-diablo-4-players-applaud-blizzard-dungeon-changes-season-2).

9. **Dark and Darker — extraction stakes make every fight matter.** Gear is equipped from your own stash before a
   run; dying loses everything carried, extracting banks it permanently. Why it works: loss aversion is a stronger
   motivator than gain-seeking — a M+ death costs a timer, a D&D death costs your build. The tension is the whole
   product. Source: [Dark and Darker proves extraction shooters don't have to be shooters at all, PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/dark-and-darker-proves-extraction-shooters-dont-have-to-be-shooters-at-all/).

10. **Deep Rock Galactic — class kit and mission type are designed to need each other.** Devs studied Team
    Fortress 2's class identity and built each of the 4 dwarf classes (Scout, Gunner, Engineer, Driller) so their
    signature tool (zipline, shield, platforms, tunnels) solves a problem the *other* classes create or can't
    solve alone, across mission types that vary which tool matters most. Why it works: synergy isn't a buff aura,
    it's terrain and mobility interacting — every class is "equally powerful" only in a full party. Source:
    [Mikkel Pedersen interview, GameRant](https://gamerant.com/deep-rock-galactic-interview-season-1-co-op-design-battle-pass-progression/); [Deep Rock Galactic CEO interview, NoFrag](https://nofrag.com/interview-discussion-with-the-ceo-of-the-studio-behind-deep-rock-galactic/).

11. **Monster Hunter — part-breaks reward precision over raw damage.** Breaking a specific monster part yields
    unique materials and changes the monster's moveset/behavior (limping, dropped armor, exposed weak point) for
    the rest of the hunt. Why it works: it creates a second objective layered on top of "kill it" that changes
    optimal play mid-fight and gives a visible, immediate reward for good target selection. Source:
    [How to Design Satisfying Gameplay Loops — Monster Hunter Analysis, Kokutech](https://www.kokutech.com/blog/gamedev/design-patterns/flow-state/monster-hunter-world); [Monster Hunter's gameplay loop, ginger.wtf](https://ginger.wtf/posts/monster-hunter-gameplay-loop/).

## What players hate

- **Lost Ark's Chaos Dungeons are a chore, not content.** Players call the daily mechanic "boring," say it's "so
  simple" for something so central to progression, and the repetitive, bot-friendly design is widely read as
  deliberately tedious to push cash-shop skips. Source: [Steam Community discussion thread, Lost Ark](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1599340/discussions/0/3409802983880951621); [Lost Ark botting issue, ZLeague](https://www.zleague.gg/theportal/lost-ark-chronicles-sounding-the-alarm-on-a-hidden-botting-issue/).
- **Diablo IV's pre-rework Nightmare Dungeons wasted time on backtracking.** Objectives spread far apart with
  confusing maps meant, in players' words, dungeon objectives existed "just to waste your time" with zero reward
  for the detour. Source: ["The codex is easier to fill up", Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/mmo/the-codex-easier-fill-up-diablo-4-players-applaud-blizzard-dungeon-changes-season-2).
- **Monster Hunter World's grind is real, and some players bounce off it.** Getting best-in-slot gear means
  re-running the same hunt for low-drop-rate materials; not every player buys the "it's skill, not grind" defense.
  Source: [Repetitive grind got me down, GameFAQs boards](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/211368-monster-hunter-world/76556898); [Monster Hunter World: My Broken Loop, Dying Pixels](https://dyingpixels.wordpress.com/2018/10/08/monster-hunter-world-my-broken-loop/).
- **FFXIV's harder optional dungeon tier (Criterion) undersold.** Community discussion around Criterion's tepid
  reception suggests most players prefer easier variants and don't find a harder dungeon's rewards worth the
  friction — a caution against assuming "M+ but in FFXIV" is automatically wanted. Source: [FFXIV raiding-culture discussion, mmo-champion](https://www.mmo-champion.com/threads/2287541-WoW-players-that-switched-to-FFXIV-I-need-your-opinions/page5).
- **Dark and Darker's extraction tension is also its biggest turnoff.** The same steep loss that creates stakes
  also means a bad run can erase real time investment in seconds, which the genre itself acknowledges as a
  double-edged design. Source: [Suffers like most extraction games, Steam Community](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2016590/discussions/0/4407417835481516409/).

## Steal / adapt / avoid

**Steal** (fits the constraint set directly):
- FFXIV's visible, learnable telegraphs (ground AoE + numbered head-markers) — directly compatible with top-down
  2D, no-art rendering: shapes on the ground *are* the mechanic.
- Monster Hunter's part-break as a secondary objective — cheap to add on a 2D boss with hitbox zones, rewards
  precision inside a ≤5-ability kit without adding a 6th button.
- Lost Ark's stagger bar as a shared party win-condition — reuses existing damage numbers as a second resource,
  no new keybind required.

**Adapt** (needs reshaping for ≤5 abilities / ≤10 keybinds / synergy-first):
- GW2 combo fields → scale down to 2–3 field types total (not GW2's ~5x5 field/finisher matrix) so synergy is
  learnable inside a 10-keybind budget, and make every class carry one field OR one finisher, never both.
- DRG's terrain-based class interdependence → without terrain tools per class (no zipline/tunnel budget), express
  this instead through the ability kit itself: one class opens a window (stagger, expose, root) that only another
  class's kit is built to punish.
- Dark and Darker's stakes → too harsh wholesale for a POC; adapt as "the timer/wipe has a cost beyond restart"
  (e.g., partial gear-screen setback) rather than full loot loss.

**Avoid**:
- Lost Ark's Chaos Dungeon repetition-as-chore — never let the core loop read as a dev-intended skip target.
- Diablo IV's pre-fix backtracking — dungeon/objective layout must keep the party moving toward the fight, not
  through empty rooms.
- FFXIV Criterion's lesson: don't assume a harder optional mode is wanted by default — Mythic+-style escalating
  difficulty needs to earn its audience with a good base loop first.

## Verdict

Keyrun should steal FFXIV-style visible telegraphs (ground shapes read as the mechanic, no art needed) and Monster
Hunter-style part-breaks as a free secondary objective, then build synergy the GW2/DRG way: a small, closed set of
field/finisher-style interactions where one class's ability opens a window only another class's kit can capitalize
on, scaled to 2–3 field types so it's learnable inside 10 keybinds. Treat the dungeon layout like Diablo IV's
post-fix design — no backtracking, constant forward pressure — and give death/timeout a real cost without going
full Dark-and-Darker loss. This is the fun WoW under-builds: telegraphed puzzles, systemic synergy, permanent
consequence, zero dead time.
