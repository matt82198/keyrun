- MPLUS-PATROLS one patrol pack walks a fixed waypoint loop along corridor-2 between the Choir and the Pair (Matt 2026-10-20 "lean into m+ design heavily while in the dungeon"; BIBLE 9.1 "it does not move unless it is a patrol"): layout.json `patrols` (`LayoutPatrol`, validated fail-closed by `LayoutValidator.CheckPatrols`: axis-aligned legs each inside one box, positive speed and radius, unique non-pull id, members with a forces value) is turned into sim `PatrolDef`s by `NavLayouts.ToSimPatrols` and armed by HostRunSetup (`DungeonDef.Patrols`); `Encounter/Patrols.cs` gives the patrol point as a pure integer function of the tick (`PointAt`, tick * speed mod loop length), `Pulls.SpawnAllIdle` places members idle on the loop, `RunRoot.StepLayout` walks idle members (`Patrols.Walk`) before the aggro check, and `Aggro.GroupsOf` adds each patrol as a pack group (pull index -1, ring at that tick's point, `pack_engage`), so a party in its ring engages it and a patrol walking into a fight joins it; it never moves the pull index and banks forces like any pack (`game/sim/Keyrun.Sim.Tests/Encounter/PatrolTests.cs`, `game/content/Keyrun.Content.Tests/LayoutPatrolTests.cs`, `game/bots/Keyrun.Bots.Tests/ShippedPatrolTests.cs`).
BUILDLOG: feat/mplus-patrols adds a corridor-2 patrol pack on a deterministic tick-based waypoint loop that engages on the prespawned packs' aggro rule and joins a fight it walks into, because Matt asked for the dungeon to lean into Mythic+ design and a pack the party must time or avoid is the core of M+ routing.
